Important disclosures

SmartTrades is a research and information product for self-directed investors.

Not investment advice

Content, rankings and scores are informational and educational only. They are not individualized investment recommendations or offers to buy or sell securities.

SEC filing data

The free beta derives metrics from standardized facts reported by public companies through SEC EDGAR. Filings can be amended or restated, XBRL tagging differs among issuers, and calculations can contain errors. Verify material facts independently.

Ranking scope

A Top 10 designation means Top 10 within the current SmartTrades coverage universe and methodology—not the entire investable market. Coverage can exclude companies when comparable standardized inputs are unavailable.

No live valuation in V0.2

The free beta intentionally excludes current stock prices, valuation multiples, analyst estimates and momentum until an appropriate licensed market-data source is integrated.

Performance

High scores do not guarantee future performance. Historical financial quality can deteriorate and markets can price securities differently from fundamental models.
