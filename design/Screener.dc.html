Rules every stock must pass

Yield is compared to each company's own 5-year history, not a fixed number. That separates a quality name on sale from a stock that always yields a lot.

  • 7+ years of dividend raises in a row
  • No dividend cut in 10 years
  • Pays out under 65% of earnings
  • Pays out under 70% of free cash flow
  • Net debt under 3.5× EBITDA
  • Interest covered more than 4×
Yield-trap filter. Any stock whose forward earnings estimates fell more than 20% in six months is removed — that high yield is likely a warning, not a bargain.
