Methodology

SmartTrades is a rules-based research system. Scores are designed to organize financial information—not to predict guaranteed returns or tell any individual investor what to buy.

Smart Score

The general-purpose score combines five pillars.

Business quality30%
Growth20%
Valuation20%
Financial strength20%
Momentum / confirmation10%

What the pillars measure

Quality: returns on capital/equity, margins, cash conversion and consistency.

Growth: multi-period revenue, EPS and free-cash-flow growth.

Valuation: earnings, free-cash-flow and enterprise-value multiples, normalized relative where historical context is available.

Financial strength: leverage, liquidity and interest coverage.

Momentum: medium-term price and estimate/fundamental trend inputs when licensed data supports them.

Percentile scoring

Most continuous factors are winsorized to reduce outlier distortion, then converted into 0–100 percentile ranks within an appropriate comparison universe. Where sensible, sector-relative comparisons are used so asset-light software firms are not naively compared with banks or utilities on the wrong metric.

Eligibility

Rankings can exclude stocks with insufficient history, missing filings, extreme financial distress, micro-cap liquidity, non-operating structures or metrics that are not comparable. Exact filters are versioned in the generated dataset.

Ranking-specific models

The Dividend Growth list emphasizes payout coverage and dividend history. Compounders emphasize returns on capital and cash generation. Quality on Sale gives substantially more weight to valuation. Each ranking page publishes its weighting.

Data provenance

SmartTrades stores the source and timestamp of each refresh. SEC filings may be used for raw reported fundamentals; market prices, corporate actions, analyst estimates and standardized fundamentals require an appropriately licensed market-data provider when displayed commercially.
