About SmartTrades

Better filters, not fortune telling.

SmartTrades is being built as a transparent quantitative research system for self-directed investors. The free beta begins with public SEC filing data and deliberately avoids pretending that unavailable data is live.

What it does now

Ranks a focused universe of large U.S. operating companies by quality, growth, financial strength, cash generation and dividend growth.

What comes later

Licensed valuation data, broader coverage, historical ranking changes, watchlists, alerts and premium research tools—only after the underlying data architecture is reliable.
