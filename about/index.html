About SmartTrades

Better filters, not fortune telling.

SmartTrades exists to make quantitative stock research understandable. The product organizes public-company information into transparent rankings so investors can narrow thousands of securities into a more useful research queue.

What it is

Rules-based stock screening, factor scoring, valuation context, watchlists and ranking-change detection.

What it is not

A promise of returns, personalized investment advice, a robo-adviser, an auto-trading service or a system that claims AI can know tomorrow’s price.
